# Frequently asked questions

## What is Nekomo?
Nekomo is a community-driven anime and manga client that combines supported catalogues, user-added sources, and viewing or reading features.

## Does Nekomo include sources?
No sources are installed or bundled. You add them yourself in Settings > Extensions, either from any repository URL you trust or from the optional official repository of free and legal sources (nothing is installed until you add it and confirm). APK extensions (Aniyomi, Mihon) are never installed or updated automatically.

## Why is a source not working?
External sources are independently maintained and may become unavailable or incompatible after upstream changes. Check the source's official release information and the [troubleshooting guide](guides/troubleshooting.md).

## Why are titles or artwork missing?
Metadata and content sources are separate systems. Check the selected metadata provider and try an alternate title.

## Where can I report a bug?
Use the [main Nekomo issue tracker](https://github.com/Nekomo-App/Nekomo/issues). Include app version, Android version, reproduction steps, and logs with secrets and personal information removed.

## How do updates to sources work?
Settings > Extensions has an automatic updater (on or off, frequency, manual check, history). Updates only replace the old version after a verified download; if one fails the old version stays and the error is shown.

## Why does a title open without any episodes to stream?
Details come from metadata services, streaming comes from the sources you install. With no sources the app works as a tracker; pick a source in the Sources dropdown when you have one.

## Which APK is the TV version?
Every Android TV build has TV in its name, for example `Nekomo-TV-release.apk`. See the build table in the getting started guide.

## What is NEX?
NEX (Nekomo Extension Exchange) is a companion app that manages repositories and extensions for Nekomo, Mihon, Aniyomi, CloudStream and Zangetsu. See [NEX](https://github.com/Nekomo-App/NEX).
