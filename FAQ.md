# Frequently asked questions

## What is Nekomo?
Nekomo is a community-driven anime and manga client that combines supported catalogues, user-added sources, and viewing or reading features.

## Does Nekomo include sources?
No. Third-party sources and extension packages are not bundled. Users add them separately and should use trusted developer channels.

## Why is a source not working?
External sources are independently maintained and may become unavailable or incompatible after upstream changes. Check the source's official release information and the [troubleshooting guide](guides/troubleshooting.md).

## Why are titles or artwork missing?
Metadata and content sources are separate systems. Check the selected metadata provider and try an alternate title.

## Where can I report a bug?
Use the [main Nekomo issue tracker](https://github.com/Nekomo-App/Nekomo/issues). Include app version, Android version, reproduction steps, and logs with secrets and personal information removed.
