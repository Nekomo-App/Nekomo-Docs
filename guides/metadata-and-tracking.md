# Metadata and tracking

Metadata describes a title independently of the source used to access its episodes or chapters.

Depending on the app version and configuration, metadata may include:

- Main and alternate titles
- Synopsis and release information
- Posters, covers, and other artwork
- Genres and status
- External catalogue identifiers
- Watch or reading progress through supported tracking services

## Metadata versus content sources

A content source may provide incomplete or inconsistent metadata. Keeping catalogue metadata separate from content resolution helps the app display title information even when a particular source does not provide all fields.

## Tracking

Tracking providers are external services. Their availability, supported fields, and authentication flows depend on the app version and provider APIs. Review each provider's privacy information before connecting an account.
