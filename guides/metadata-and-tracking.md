# Metadata and tracking

Metadata describes a title independently of the source used to access episodes or chapters. Depending on the app version and configuration, metadata may include titles, alternate titles, synopsis, release information, artwork, genres, status, and external catalogue identifiers.

## Metadata versus content sources

A content source may provide incomplete metadata. Keeping catalogue metadata separate from content resolution helps the app display title information even when a particular source does not provide every field.

## Tracking

Tracking providers are external services. Availability, supported fields, and authentication flows depend on the app version and provider APIs. Review each provider's privacy information before connecting an account.
