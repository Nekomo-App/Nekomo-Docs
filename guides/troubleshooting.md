# Troubleshooting

## The app will not start
- Confirm the build is intended for your Android version and device.
- Restart the app and device.
- Check release notes for known issues.

## A source is missing
- Confirm it was added using the correct method for its ecosystem.
- Check official documentation and version requirements.
- Confirm the format is supported by your Nekomo version.

## Content will not load
- Check your network connection.
- Determine whether the issue affects one source or multiple sources.
- Check whether the source is still maintained and available.

## Artwork or metadata is missing
- Try an alternate or original title.
- Check the selected metadata provider and catalogue settings.
- Remember that metadata and content sources are separate.

## Reporting a bug
Include Nekomo version, Android version/device, reproduction steps, expected and actual behavior, and relevant logs. Remove passwords, tokens, cookies, and personal information.
