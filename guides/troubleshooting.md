# Troubleshooting

## The app will not start

- Confirm that the build is intended for your Android version and device.
- Restart the app and device.
- Check the project's release notes for known issues.

## A source is missing

- Confirm that it was added or installed using the correct method for its ecosystem.
- Check its official documentation and version requirements.
- Confirm that the source format is supported by your Nekomo version.

## Content will not load

- Check your network connection.
- Determine whether the issue affects one source or multiple sources.
- Check whether the source is still maintained and available.
- Review any source-specific requirements.

## Artwork or metadata is missing

- Try searching by an alternate or original title.
- Check the selected metadata provider and catalogue settings.
- Remember that metadata and content sources are separate.

## Reporting a bug

Include Nekomo version, Android version/device, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, actual behavior, and relevant logs. Remove passwords, access tokens, session cookies, and personal information before sharing.
